About the MLM Savings Scheme

The MLM Savings Scheme is a simple referral-based earning and savings model that helps members register, pay through UPI, and track their joining plan from a clear dashboard view.

Members can choose between three levels based on the joining amount and corresponding commission: ₹100 with 5%, ₹500 with 3%, and ₹1000 with 2%.
